## B2d — FP8 vs FP16 A/B (both FI-on)

### Timing (ms)

| Region | fp8_fi_on p50 | fp16_ab p50 | Δ | fp8_fi_on p95 | fp16_ab p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `unet_step` | **27.950** | **29.799** | −1.849 (−6.2%) | 29.636 | 30.752 |
| `unet_step.engine` | 27.735 | 29.571 | −1.836 (−6.2%) | 29.388 | 30.472 |
| `encode_image` | 1.181 | 1.167 | ≈0 | 1.703 | 1.950 |
| `decode_image` | 1.117 | 1.105 | ≈0 | 1.316 | 1.162 |

**FP8 is ~1.85 ms (6.2%) faster per denoise step than the batch-identical fp16 twin.**
Encode/decode are precision-independent (tiny-VAE unchanged) and act as a cross-arm
control — they agree to within noise, confirming the arms are otherwise identical.

### Kernel-family GPU-time shares (`logs/nsys_*_20260729_cuda_gpu_kern_sum.csv`)

| Family | fp8_fi_on | fp16_ab | fp8_fi_off |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| fp8 GEMM/conv (`sm89_xmma_*_e4m3*`) | 36.3% | — | 38.7% |
| f16 GEMM (cutlass/xmma, non-e4m3) | 32.4% | 67.8% | 33.0% |
| elementwise/norm | 11.4% | 10.9% | 11.7% |
| attention (fmha) | 1.6% | 1.6% | 1.8% |
| other (cat/copy/misc) | 18.2% | 19.7% | 14.8% |
| total captured kernel time | 123.4 ms | 128.9 ms | 116.5 ms |

Key structural finding: **only about half of the GEMM time actually migrated to fp8
kernels.** The fp8 engine still spends 32.4% of its kernel time in f16 GEMMs — the report
§3.1 dominant kernel (`cutlass_80_wmma …128x2`) is still the #2 kernel at 13.1% in the fp8
arm (vs 12.9% in fp16). Its top kernel is `sm89_xmma_gemm_e4m3f16…64x128x64` at 14.8%.
This is why the end-to-end fp8 win is a modest 6.2% rather than the ~2× a fully-quantized
GEMM path would suggest. (The 2026-07-10 audit quoted ≈45% GEMM share; this capture puts
true-e4m3 kernels at 36.3% with another 32.4% still f16 — aggregation differs, both
confirm GEMM dominance.)

### Output quality (fixed seed, deterministic input)

`logs/quality_check_20260729.py`: identical structured input (gradients + checker), seed
2416333, 12 identical frames per arm so V2V temporal state settles, final frame compared
(`logs/quality_fp8_20260729.npy` vs `logs/quality_fp16_20260729.npy`):

- **max-abs-diff = 205.0, MAE = 8.248, PSNR = 23.65 dB**

This is a cross-precision comparison (fp8 vs fp16 output, not vs ground truth). 23.65 dB
means a visible distribution shift, not noise-level divergence — expected, since fp8
quantizes ~3900 QDQ nodes and TRT fuses a different graph. No blockiness/banding artifact
class was flagged; treat as "different sample from the same distribution".

### 3c decision input (recorded for the deferred adoption decision)

fp8 buys **6.2% p50 latency** on `unet_step` at the cost of a **23.65 dB PSNR** deviation
from fp16 output. The latency ceiling is limited by the ~32% of GEMM time that stays f16
(see above) — raising fp8 layer coverage is the lever that would change this trade-off.

## B2a — Feature-injection ablation gate (both fp8)

### Timing (ms)

| Region | fp8_fi_on p50 | fp8_fi_off p50 | Δ (FI cost) | fp8_fi_on p95 | fp8_fi_off p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `unet_step` | **27.950** | **25.118** | +2.832 (+11.3%) | 29.636 | 26.259 |
| `unet_step.engine` | 27.735 | 24.888 | +2.847 (+11.4%) | 29.388 | 25.770 |
| `encode_image` | 1.181 | 1.171 | ≈0 | | |
| `decode_image` | 1.117 | 1.114 | ≈0 | | |

### Gate verdict: **GO**

Plan rule (`PMPP_Follow_Up.md` B2a): `unet_step` p50 delta < 2% (≈0.4 ms) ⇒ no-go on the
3a fused-kernel plugin; ≥ 3–5% ⇒ go. Measured FI cost is **+2.832 ms = +11.3% over the
FI-off baseline** (p95: +3.377 ms, +12.9%) — several times past the go threshold.
**Verdict: GO** on 3a, with the plan's proviso: targeted ncu on the FI-attributable
kernels first, before writing any plugin.

Where the cost lives (kernel-family deltas, FI-on minus FI-off, absolute captured time):
GEMM families are flat (e4m3 44.8 → 45.1 ms, f16 cutlass 40.0 → 38.5 ms), while the
"other" family (cat/copy/pointwise — `CatArrayBatchedCopy` is 4.8–5.0% in both fp8 arms)
grows from 17.2 to 22.4 ms. The FI cost signature is **elementwise/copy overhead, not
GEMM** — exactly the class of work a fused-kernel plugin targets, which supports the GO.

### Pass 2 — `--kernel-regex "e4m3"`: the TRT fp8 kernels

Rep `logs/ncu_config_fp8_fi_on_detailed_20260729_130544.ncu-rep`, export

| Kernel (launches) | Grid | regs limit | smem limit | regs/thread | max theor. warps % |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `sm89_xmma_gemm_e4m3f16…64x64x64_stage6` (4/10) | (32,10,1) | 4 | **2** | 102 | 16.7 |
| `sm89_xmma_gemm_e4m3e4m3…64x64x64_stage4` (2/10) | (32,10,1) | 4 | **3** | 126 | 25.0 |
| `sm89_xmma_fprop_implicit_gemm_e4m3f16…64x64x64` conv (2/10) | (10,32,1) | 5 | **3** | 90 | 25.0 |
| `sm89_xmma_fprop_implicit_gemm_e4m3e4m3…64x64x64` conv (1/10) | (10,32,1) | 4 | **3** | 120 | 25.0 |
| `sm89_xmma_gemm_e4m3f16…32x64x64_stage5` (1/10) | (5,10,1) | 6 | **3** | 76 | 25.0 |

(bold = binding limiter, blocks/SM)

**Every captured e4m3 kernel is shared-memory-limited**: the smem limit (2–3 blocks/SM)
is strictly below the register limit (4–6) on all 10 launches, capping max theoretical
warps at 16.7–25%. Registers/thread are 76–126 — nowhere near the PMPP §15.7
register-tiled profile (~255). The report's smem-limited occupancy thesis therefore
extends from the fp16 engine to the fp8 TRT kernels as well. Grid sizes (50–320 blocks
at 2–3 resident blocks/SM on 128 SMs) also keep these kernels in the ≤~1-wave regime the
2026-05-24 roofline pass flagged (`docs/profiling/unet_ncu_roofline_2026-05-24.md`,
wave-limited at 0.2–0.4 waves) — small grids and smem-capped residency compound.

## Latent engine-hash bug (recorded, not fixed)

`wrapper.py:2102` feeds the **raw** `use_feature_injection` flag into
`EngineManager.get_engine_path`, while the builder applies the **effective** flag
`use_feature_injection AND use_cached_attn` (`models.py:514`). Two configs that differ
only in FI while `use_cached_attn` is false therefore produce different hashes for
byte-identical engines — a spurious-rebuild bug, not a correctness bug. Recorded here per
plan; not fixed this pass.
